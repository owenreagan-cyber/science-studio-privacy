Science Studio Google Drive & Google Slides integration

Science Studio is a local, educator-operated production tool. This policy explains what the Google integration accesses, why it needs it, where data is stored, and how to delete it or revoke access.

Effective: October 28, 2026 Version 1.0 Applies to: Science Studio integration and this website

The short version

  • The integration runs locally under the Google account you authorize.
  • It uses Google data only to create, convert, verify, and optionally add approved media to presentations you choose.
  • It does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train generalized AI models.
  • It does not send Google user data to Science Studio servers; we do not operate one for this integration.
  • OAuth credentials and small state files stay on your computer, outside the project repository.
  • It does not delete Drive files automatically. You control deletion and can revoke access at any time.

1. Scope and roles

This policy applies to the Science Studio Google Drive and Google Slides integration (the “integration”) and to this website. It does not apply to Google Drive, Google Slides, GitHub, or any other third-party service. Those services have their own privacy policies and terms.

The educator who authorizes the integration controls the local installation and the Google account it uses. Science Studio does not operate a hosted service that receives the contents of your Drive or Slides files. The integration communicates with Google directly from the local machine.

In this policy, “we” and “Science Studio” refer to the independent Science Studio project that maintains this integration and website. “Google user data” means information the integration receives from Google APIs.

Science Studio is an independent project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Google.

2. Who it is for

Science Studio is intended for educators, curriculum producers, and other adults who prepare instructional materials. It is not directed to children or students, does not create student accounts, and does not ask for student information.

Do not use the integration to upload student education records, individualized education programs, medical information, disciplinary records, or other sensitive student data. If such content is uploaded by mistake, delete it from Google Drive and from any local reports or thumbnails, following the steps in Deletion and revocation.

3. Google API scopes

Google authorizes access through OAuth 2.0 scopes. The integration requests the following scopes. Google may describe them differently on its consent screen; these are the scopes configured in the integration.

Google API scopes used by the Science Studio integration
Scope What it allows How the integration uses it
drive.file See, create, and manage only the specific Google Drive files this integration creates, or that you explicitly open with it. It does not grant access to your entire Drive. Create a dedicated staging folder, upload and convert approved source files, set app properties used for idempotency, and read back or update files the integration manages.
presentations.readonly View Google Slides presentations and their content. Read slide structure, text, speaker notes, revision metadata, and thumbnails for verification. Only presentations identified by ID are requested.
presentations View and edit Google Slides presentations. Used only by the optional media inserter, to add an approved video object to an allowlisted staged deck and verify the result. The basic publisher does not request this scope.
openid and userinfo.email Confirm the signed-in Google account and its email address. Check that the authorized account matches the expected educator account before a write. The email is used for this local check and may be present in the local OAuth token. It is not sent to Science Studio.

Authorization is optional. If you do not authorize the integration, it cannot access Google data. You can revoke access at any time; see Deletion and revocation.

4. Data accessed

Depending on the commands you run, the integration may access:

  • Account identity: the email address and basic OpenID identity of the Google account you authorize.
  • File metadata: file and folder IDs, names, MIME types, parent folders, timestamps, app properties, and links for files the integration creates or manages.
  • File content you choose to upload: presentation files, video files, and poster images supplied to an explicit publish or media command.
  • Presentation content: slide structure, slide text, speaker notes, revision IDs, and rendered thumbnails of presentations identified for verification.
  • OAuth credentials: access tokens, refresh tokens, granted scopes, and possibly an ID token containing the authorized account's email.

The integration does not collect data from your Google account in the background or on a schedule. Access happens when you run a command that needs it.

Do not upload content you are not authorized to use. You are responsible for the permissions, licenses, and consent associated with the files you publish.

5. Files created or changed

Writes are explicit. The publisher is read-only by default, and a Drive write requires an --execute flag. The main operations are:

  • Staging a presentation. The integration creates or reuses a dedicated staging folder, uploads an approved presentation file, converts it to a native Google Slides presentation, and records a source hash, run identifier, and timestamp in the file's app properties. If the same source hash was already staged, the existing copy is reused instead of creating a duplicate.
  • Inserting approved media. The optional media inserter can upload an approved video or poster as a file created by the integration, insert a video object into an allowlisted staged deck, and verify that the object is present.
  • Owner-run deployment updates. Separate deployment scripts, run explicitly by the owner, may update files that the same tooling created or manages. Those scripts back up the previous content and verify the result; they do not scan for unrelated files.

The integration does not automatically promote a staged deck into a live classroom deck. Promotion is a separate owner-controlled decision and is not implemented in the publisher. The integration does not contain automated delete operations, and it does not empty your trash.

6. Local storage

The integration is local-first. It stores a small amount of data on the computer where it runs, outside the project repository. The integration writes its token and state files with owner-only file permissions; keep the configuration folder protected.

Data stored locally by the Science Studio integration
Data Where Why
OAuth client secret Local configuration folder outside the repository Identifies the desktop app to Google during authorization.
OAuth tokens Local configuration folder outside the repository Avoid repeated sign-in. May contain access and refresh tokens, granted scopes, and the authorized account's email.
Publisher state Local configuration folder outside the repository Maps a source file hash to the staged Drive file ID, display name, and run identifier so repeat runs can be idempotent.
Verification reports A local output location you choose Record presentation ID, title, revision ID, source hash, and structural check results.
Slide thumbnails A local output folder, if thumbnail rendering is used Images of slides for visual review. Delete them when they are no longer needed.

Tokens are not encrypted by the integration itself. They rely on your operating system's account security and owner-only file permissions. Treat the token files as passwords. Protect the device and revoke access if it is lost or compromised. The integration does not write Google user data into the public website repository.

Command output may include local file paths and publish-plan details on your screen. The integration does not upload that console output anywhere.

7. Retention

There is no automatic retention period and no scheduled cleanup. Local credentials, state, verification reports, and thumbnails remain on the computer until you delete them. Staged presentations and uploaded media remain in Google Drive until you delete them there, subject to Google's own retention and backup practices.

Uninstalling the tool does not automatically delete Drive files or the local configuration folder. Use the steps in Deletion and revocation to remove both.

8. Sharing and third parties

The integration communicates directly with Google Drive and Google Slides APIs over HTTPS. It does not route Google user data through a Science Studio server, and we do not operate a server for this integration.

For Google user data received through this integration:

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade Google user data.
  • We do not use Google user data for advertising or ad targeting.
  • We do not use Google user data to train generalized AI or machine learning models.
  • We do not share Google user data with third parties except: with Google as necessary to perform the operation you requested; with your explicit consent; or when required by law, regulation, or a valid legal process, or to protect safety and security.

If you export, share, or open a file with another tool yourself, that action is outside this policy. The third party's terms apply to it.

Google may process data in countries other than yours under its own safeguards and terms. The local integration itself does not transfer data internationally except by calling Google's APIs.

9. This website

This is a static informational website. It has no user accounts, sign-in, forms, or comment features. We do not add cookies, analytics, or advertising trackers to it.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process standard technical information — such as IP address, user agent, and request time — to serve the site, maintain security, and diagnose problems. That processing is governed by GitHub's privacy statement, not this policy. Links to Google and GitHub take you to services with their own policies.

10. Security

  • OAuth 2.0 installed-app authorization; the integration does not ask for or store your Google password.
  • OAuth client secrets are kept outside the project repository by default; tokens and state files are written with owner-only file permissions.
  • Least-privilege scopes, read-only defaults, allowlists, and explicit execute steps limit what a run can touch.
  • The authorized account is checked before a write.
  • No service accounts, impersonation, or domain-wide delegation are used.
  • HTTPS is used for all calls to Google APIs.

No security measure is perfect. If a device is lost, a token is exposed, or you suspect unauthorized access, revoke the integration's access in your Google Account and delete the local credentials promptly.

11. Deletion and revocation

You can remove access and data without contacting us. The integration does not currently include an in-app disconnect button.

  1. Revoke Google access. Open your Google Account's third-party apps and services page, select Science Studio, and remove access. Revocation stops future API access, but it does not delete files that were already created in Drive.
  2. Delete Google Drive data. In Google Drive, delete the dedicated staging folder and any uploaded media you no longer need. Empty the trash if you want permanent deletion under your Google account settings.
  3. Delete local data. Remove the integration's local configuration folder, including its OAuth client secret, tokens, and state file. Also delete any verification reports and thumbnails you created. The tool does not do this automatically.
  4. No separate server copy. Because we do not operate a server for this integration, there is no Science Studio account or server-side copy to delete. If you believe we hold data about you, contact us using the method below.

Google's own retention, backup, and deletion practices apply to data held in your Google account. We do not control those practices.

12. Google API Limited Use

Limited Use statement. Science Studio's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

In particular, for Google user data received through this integration:

  • We use it only to provide the publishing, conversion, verification, and optional media-insertion features described in this policy.
  • We do not sell it or use it for advertising.
  • We do not use it to train generalized AI or machine learning models.
  • We do not transfer it to third parties except as necessary to provide the integration, with your explicit consent, or as required by law or for security and safety.
  • We do not allow humans to read it, except the local user operating the tool, or as required for security, legal compliance, or with your affirmative consent.

The integration accesses only the Google data needed for the operation you request. It does not use Google user data to build profiles, score people, or serve ads.

13. Children and student data

Science Studio is an educator tool, not a student-facing service. It does not collect information from children, create student profiles, or require students to sign in. Educators must not use the integration to upload student education records or other sensitive student information. If student data is uploaded by mistake, remove it from Google Drive and from local reports or thumbnails as described above.

14. Your choices and rights

You choose whether to authorize the integration, which files to publish, and when to revoke access. Because the integration stores data locally and in your own Google account, you can access, export, correct, or delete most of it directly using Google Drive and your computer's file system.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or port personal data. You can exercise those choices through the steps above. If you need help, contact us without including personal, student, or credential information.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the integration, legal requirements, or Google's policies. The effective date at the top of this page will change when we do. Material changes will be posted on this page. Because the integration is installed locally, you are responsible for updating your copy to receive corresponding changes.

16. Contact

Questions or concerns about this policy can be raised through the public issue tracker for this website: github.com/owenreagan-cyber/science-studio-privacy/issues.

Do not include student information, school records, personal contact details, OAuth tokens, client secrets, or other credentials in a public issue. For a security report, describe the issue without posting the sensitive value.
